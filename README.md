# 🌳 Above the Canopy

**An interactive animation about the difference between leadership, management and team members.**

A large Australian forest with a tall canopy. Far to the east, a glowing golden pyramid shimmers above the trees: that's the **Leader's vision** of the goal. It isn't built yet. At the end of the journey the koalas **build the pyramid themselves**, course by course, until it fills the vision and turns solid.
Time is measured like a real project, in **days**: the top bar counts *Day 1, Day 2...* until the koalas deliver.

| Role | Character | Where they are | What they can see | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leader** | 🦅 Eagle | Flies *above* the canopy | Where the team is now, and holds the vision of the goal (the golden blueprint of the pyramid) | **Vision, priorities, backing the team & adapting.** Sets the destination, filters competing priorities, says YES to what the team needs (and protects their time), changes the plan when better information comes up from the ground |
| **Manager** | 🦉 Owl | Flies *below* the canopy, above the team | The obstacles ahead | **Planning, removing obstacles, communication up & down, coaching & delegating.** Scouts ahead, warns the team, coaches and delegates, passes ideas up, calls breaks, recognises wins, coordinates with other managers |
| **Team** | 🐨 Four koalas: Kip, Banjo, Matilda & Wattle | On the forest floor, cutting through the bush | Only a few metres ahead | **Execution, problem-solving & leading from the ground.** The everyday work, gathering materials, learning new skills, solving obstacles, reporting surprises, and spotting better ways to do things |

![The koalas hacking through the bush, the owl scouting, the eagle above the canopy](docs/koalas-hacking-bushes.png)

## The teaching idea

Nobody can see the whole picture on their own:

- The **leader** holds the vision of the goal but can't see the boulders.
- The **manager** can see the boulders but not the goal.
- The **team** can see neither. They're down in the bush doing the work.

The journey works when vision flows down (leader ➜ manager), warnings and plans flow down to the team (manager ➜ team), and requests and progress flow back up (team ➜ manager ➜ leader).
Use the **Perspective** buttons to see the world through each role's eyes (each view also shows only that role's speech bubbles and narration, so you can rewatch with one perspective to focus on that role's communication), and turn **Manager communication OFF**
to watch the same koalas in the same forest get surprised, take wrong turns and take much longer (about 202 days instead of about 157), and even build the wrong shape.

## What happens on the journey

**The everyday work.** Between the big obstacles, the koalas keep stopping to cut through small bushes on the path: little pruning saws swinging, leaves flying.
That's the constant day-to-day work that nobody above the canopy sees.

**Eight obstacles,** each one different, each with a warned solution and a "nobody told us" version:

| # | Obstacle | When the Manager warned them | When communication is OFF |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🌿 Tangle of vines | Arrive with cutting tools and cut straight through | Get stuck, run all the way back for better tools |
| 2 | 🪨 Giant boulder | **Upskill:** a quick hammer-and-chisel training session, then break the rock down together | Bonk into it, push, back-track, then teach themselves by trial and error (slowly) |
| 3 | 🌊 Fast river | **Gather** logs from the bush and build a bridge | One koala wades in (splash!), then they scatter hunting for anything that floats |
| 4 | 🟫 Smelly bog | **Gather** flat stones and lay stepping stones | One koala sinks to its tummy, then they wander off for rocks and test wobbly stones |
| 5 | 🪵 Fallen giant log | Crawl through its hollow | Try to climb over it, slide off, trek along it |
| 6 | ⛰️ Cliff edge | **Gather** long vines, braid a rope, abseil at the marked spot | Wander the edge, then hunt for anything rope-like |
| 7 | 🐝 Bees' nest | Tiptoe past in silence | Stomp underneath, get chased by the swarm |
| 8 | 🧗 Rock wall | **Gather** straight poles and lash a ladder | Slide down bare-pawed, hunt for sticks, lash together a wobbly ladder |

When an obstacle is cleared, the caption says how many days it took. Unwarned obstacles also say what it would have taken
("Rock wall cleared, but going in blind took 6 days instead of about 3 days").

**Upskilling at the boulder: investing in team skills.** The boulder is too big to go around. With communication ON, the Manager's warning
comes with training: a whiteboard reads *🔨 HAMMER & CHISEL 101*, lightbulbs pop over the koalas, and they earn a 🎓 **UPSKILLED!** badge.
Then they spread around the rock and break it down together: rhythmic tapping, chips flying, cracks spreading, the boulder splitting into chunks,
then rubble they step over. With communication OFF, they bonk into it, push, back-track, and finally teach themselves (*TRIAL & ERROR...*, *SELF-TAUGHT*), much more slowly.

![Upskilled koalas break the boulder down with hammers and chisels](docs/boulder-hammer-chisel.png)

**Gathering materials.** When the koalas need logs (river bridge), flat stones (bog), planks (sinkhole), vines (cliff rope) or poles (ladder),
they fan out off the path into the bush, pick the materials up and carry them back in their arms, where a pile builds up and is used during the build.
With communication OFF they wander further, unsure what they're even looking for.

![Koalas carrying logs back to the river while the Manager meets an Ops manager owl](docs/gathering-logs-manager-meeting.png)

**Managers grow people: delegation.** At the river the owl says *"Kip, you lead the bridge build! ⭐"* (tag **DELEGATION**).

![Kip leads the bridge build](docs/delegation-kip.png)

**Leaders back the team** (tag **LEADER SUPPORT**). Twice the eagle visibly sides with the koalas:
- At the second eagle meeting, the Board asks for a picnic and another project wants to borrow two koalas. The Leader pushes back:
  *"The koalas need that time: the picnic can wait! 🛡️"*
- When the gum tree falls, the koalas ask for saws and safety gear. The Leader says *"Yes! Sending saws and safety gear down now. 🪂"*,
  and a supply crate floats down on a parachute through a gap in the canopy (🪂 **SUPPLIES ARRIVED**).

![A supply drop through a gap in the canopy](docs/leader-support-supply-drop.png)

**Team members lead too, and leaders adapt** (tags **TEAM IDEA** and **COURSE CORRECTION**). Hacking through thick lantana, Banjo spots a dry creek bed
running alongside: *"There's a shortcut along the creek bed! 💡"*. The owl takes it up to the eagle, and the eagle changes the plan from ground information:
the beam re-aims at the creek, the minimap shows the new dashed route, and the eagle says *"New info from the ground. We'll take the creek route; it saves N days."*
(N is worked out from the lantana still left, usually 4 to 6 days.) Credit flows back down: *"Creek route approved! Great idea, Banjo! 🌟"*.
The top bar counts **💡 Team ideas adopted**. With communication OFF, Banjo's idea goes nowhere ("Should we tell someone?") and the koalas hack through every lantana bush.

![The Leader adopts Banjo's creek route: the beam re-aims and the minimap updates](docs/course-correction-creek.png)

**Celebrating small wins.** After the bees, the owl names Matilda **Koala of the week** and a gum leaf drifts down onto her chest. 🌿

![Matilda gets the gum leaf: Koala of the week](docs/koala-of-the-week.png)

**Trust and wellbeing** (tag **WELLBEING**). After the toughest stretch (cliff, bees, snake, rock wall) the owl calls *"Take five, team"*.
The koalas sit down around a billy, drink water and munch eucalyptus leaves. It costs about 4 days, and the caption says why that's worth it:
a sustainable pace beats burnout. Afterwards they're ⚡ **REFRESHED**. With communication OFF, nobody calls a break: the koalas droop,
sweat drops fly, and they slow right down to the finish (😓 **EXHAUSTED**). The break days come back and more.

![A rest break: billy tea and gum leaves](docs/rest-break.png)

![Communication OFF: no break, exhausted koalas](docs/comms-off-exhausted.png)

**Reflection.** At the finish, the summary has a **💬 Reflect** button that shows three workplace questions (different ones for ON and OFF runs).

![The reflection panel](docs/reflection.png)

**Captions queue up.** Captions now wait their turn: a meeting caption stays on screen until the meeting ends, and important captions
(meetings, surprises, coaching, course corrections) are never cut off by routine narration.

**Rubble stays.** After the boulder is broken up, the rubble is swept to the side of the path and stays visible.

## New in v7.4: the Leader leads more than one team

Watching the Leader view, it could look as if the eagle spends a lot of time just flying. It doesn't: a leader is
responsible for many teams and projects at once. Now, in every stretch where the eagle has no meeting, distraction,
escalation, supply drop or message to the Manager, it sends **gold communication rings out in other directions** to
faint markers just beyond the edge of the screen: **🌲 Team North**, **📁 Project B**, **🧭 Strategy**, **🤝 Partners**,
**💰 Budget** and **🏗️ Project C**. Every few seconds a ring goes out (sometimes two at once, in different directions),
and some come back as a paler return ring with a decision or feedback, and the marker ticks ✅.

Now and then the eagle says what it's doing (*"Approving the budget for Team North ✅"*, *"Aligning Project B with the strategy 🧭"*,
*"Steering the bigger picture 🔭"*, *"Keeping our partners on board 🤝"*, *"Balancing the budget across all teams ⚖️"*,
*"Green light for Project C ✅"*), and the caption bar adds short **Big picture** notes, for example
*"High-level decisions: the Leader approves budgets and priorities for several teams at once, not just this one."*

- Shown in the 👁️ Everything and 🦅 Leader views; the Manager and Team views don't show them (they can't see above the canopy).
  In the Everything view the Big picture notes only appear while the caption bar is quiet, so they never hold up the story's own captions.
- The eagle meetings, distractions, escalations, supply drop and course correction always take over: any rings in flight fade out.
- It works with Manager communication OFF too: the Leader is still busy with the other teams.
- A very soft two-note chime goes with each ring (respects the sound toggle and volume).
- Purely presentational: the story, its timing, the day counts (about 157 days ON / 202 days OFF) and chapter jumps are exactly as before.

![The Leader sending rings to Strategy and Project B](docs/leader-outreach.png)

## New in v7: the koalas build the final product

**The vision.** The pyramid is no longer waiting at the end. From the very first moment it's the Leader's **vision**: a golden, dashed, gently
shimmering blueprint above the canopy (tagged **✨ THE VISION**). While the build site is far away it hangs on the eastern horizon, and it grows
and slides into place as the team gets closer. The Leader's beam points at it ("The vision ➜"), and in the Team perspective it's lost in the fog.

![The Leader's vision: a golden blueprint on the horizon](docs/vision-blueprint.png)

**Team huddle** (tag **TEAM HUDDLE**). While the Leader handles the last eagle meeting, the koalas stop at the build site and hold a stand-up:
they gather in a little circle, scratch a stepped triangle in the dirt and take turns: *Kip: "Okay team, next steps?"*, *Matilda: "I'll shape the stone."*,
*Banjo: "Rollers from the logs, like the bridge!"*, *Wattle: "I can haul. I've got this now!"*, then paws in: *"GO TEAM!"*.
Caption: *"The team meets to plan their next steps: teams self-organise when they understand the goal."* With communication OFF the huddle is short and confused
(*"What are we even building?"*, a wobbly box with a "?" in the dirt). Koalas never walk on the spot: whenever they wait, they stand and look around.

![The team huddle: the koalas plan their next steps around a triangle scratched in the dirt](docs/team-huddle.png)

**Building the final product** (tags **BUILD**, **SUPPORT**, **DELEGATION**, **TEAM IDEA**, **TEAMWORK**). After the rest break and the last eagle meeting,
the koalas reach the build site under the vision and build the pyramid in **6 courses**, one block at a time, along a supply line:
Matilda shapes stone at the pile, Banjo loads at the ramp foot, Wattle passes and hauls, and Kip sets each block. Every step calls back a skill from the journey,
shown as badges in the **🧰 Skills from the journey** panel:

| Skill | Where they learned it | Used on the build |
|---|---|---|
| 🧺 Gathering materials | River, bog, cliff and wall | Fetching stone for the pile |
| 🔨 Hammer & chisel | The boulder training | Shaping rough stone into blocks |
| 🪵 Planks & a ramp | The sinkhole crossing | A ramp up the side of the pyramid |
| 🤝 Teamwork & delegation | Kip's bridge, Wattle's coaching | Kip leads a section; Wattle sets the corner stones with confidence |
| 💡 Log rollers | Banjo's idea (logs, like the bridge) | Blocks ride up the ramp on rollers |
| 🪢 Ropes | The cliff | Hauling blocks up the steeper courses |
| 🪜 Ladder | The rock wall | Reaching the top course |

**The Manager supports, it doesn't take over.** The owl (tag *MANAGER · SUPPORTING*) organises the build sequence, flies stone to wherever Kip is working,
checks the work against the vision ("📐 Checked against the vision: the angles match!") and relays progress up ("Layer 3 done, halfway!", "Layer 4 done, two to go!").
Caption: *"Good managers support the work, they don't take it over."*

**The Leader protects the finish.** Mid-build, one last visiting eagle from Sales asks *"Add a gift shop to the pyramid? 🛍️"*. The Leader answers
*"After the launch! 🛡️ Let the koalas finish."* and keeps the beam on the vision.

![Mid-build: the owl brings stone and checks the plan against the vision while Kip leads a section](docs/build-manager-support.png)

**Vision becomes reality.** The golden capstone is passed up the line, all four koalas climb to the top and set it **together**, and the blueprint
turns solid with a chime: the pyramid the team built fills the Leader's vision exactly. Only then does the celebration start: confetti, fanfare,
every character, and the summary with the days. First, though, safety (tag **SAFETY**): the koalas climb back down one at a time,
over the top, down the ladder and the ramp, to the ground beside the pyramid. The construction gear is cleared away and the celebration happens on the ground
(*"Safely down, now we celebrate!"*).

![Safety first: the koalas climb back down one at a time](docs/climb-down.png)

![The capstone is set and the vision turns solid](docs/build-vision-solid.png)

**With communication OFF** the build is slower and messier: nobody says what size, so blocks come out uneven and some are thrown away;
there's no owl bringing stone; Banjo's rollers never get passed on, so every block is dragged; and because the vision never reached the koalas,
they build course 3 as wide as the one below. *"Wait... was it meant to be pointy?!"* They tear the whole course down and build it again (tag **REDO**).

![Communication OFF: the wrong-shaped course comes down](docs/comms-off-redo.png)

**Sound.** Stone thunks as blocks go down, chisel taps at the pile, rope creaks on the hauled courses, a rising motif as each course is completed,
a special chime when the vision turns solid, then the fanfare.

### 🔊 Sound
Every sound is **generated in the browser with the Web Audio API**: there are no audio files, no CDNs and no licensing issues, and it works offline.
Sound is on by default but only starts after your first tap, click or key press (browser autoplay rules; on iOS Safari it unlocks on the first touch).
**🔊 Sound on / 🔇 Sound off** (or <kbd>M</kbd>) and the volume slider are in the controls bar, and your choice is remembered on this device.
Pausing the story pauses the audio. At high speeds repeated sounds are thinned out, and every sound has its own rate limit so nothing gets spammy.
The mix is deliberately gentle for a classroom: soft envelopes, a compressor and a low-pass filter on the master output.

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| The forest | Soft wind and leaf rustle, bird chirps, a distant kookaburra-ish laugh, cicada swells, running water near the river (a trickle in the creek bed) |
| Messages | Chimes on every message pulse: falling notes for Leader ➜ Manager and Manager ➜ Team, a rising arpeggio for escalations up the chain |
| Leader & Manager | A soft eagle cry and wing flaps; owl hoots when the Manager spots a hazard and when a visiting owl arrives; a whoosh when visiting eagles fly in |
| Work | Saw rasps for bushes, rhythmic hammer-and-chisel taps at the boulder, log and plank thunks, stone clacks in the bog, rope creaks at the cliff, vine snips |
| Distractions | Magpie squawk, an email ping storm, thunder and a wind gust, a mozzie whine, a calendar ding for the meeting request, a branch snag |
| Surprises | An alert sting, plus a sinkhole rumble, a tree creak and crash, or a snake hiss |
| Obstacles (comms OFF) | A splash in the river, a squelch in the bog, a bonk on the boulder, the angry bees' swarm, and an echo when a call goes unanswered |
| Support & growth | Parachute whoosh and a thud when the supply drop lands; sparkles for Upskilled, coached Wattle, Kip's lead, Banjo's idea and Koala of the week; a soft chord when the Leader backs the team |

| Finish | A small fanfare and confetti pops |

## More of the journey

**Distractions happen, so refocus.** The Leader and the Manager get distracted too, veer off course, then refocus on the goal.
Each distraction is narrated, and the top bar counts **Distractions handled**:

- 🦅 Leader: swooping magpies, an "URGENT!!!" reply-all email storm, a storm front and wind gust.
- 🦉 Manager: a "Sync about the sync" meeting request, a cloud of mozzies, a snag of branches and vines.

![A magpie swoops the eagle](docs/leader-distraction-magpie.png)

**Manager meetings: sideways coordination.** Three times, while the koalas are busy gathering materials, another owl flies in under the canopy
for a short manager-level meeting (caption tag **MANAGER MEETING**). A dashed 🤝 link joins the two owls while they talk, then the visitor flies off
and the Manager refocuses on the team (a green line re-locks onto the koalas). The top bar counts **🤝 Manager syncs**.

| Visiting owl | Topic | The conversation |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Ops manager | Sharing resources | "Your team needs logs? We have spare!" · "Yes please! Drop them by the river." |
| ⚪ PMO | Status sync | "Status sync?" · "River done, bog in progress. On track." · "Great, I'll update the roadmap." |
| 🟠 Project manager B | Coordinating between teams | "Can we share your rope after?" · "Sure, once my koalas are down the cliff." · "Need a ladder later?" |

**Lots of communication.** The leader checks in with the manager and gets progress reports after every obstacle.
The manager briefs the koalas and checks on them. The koalas ask for things ("Need more rope!", "Pass more stones down the line!") and get answers.
Wins get recognised ("Great work, koalas!") and celebrated. With communication OFF, the koalas' calls go unanswered ("Is anyone up there?").

![Communication OFF: the koalas walk under the bees' nest](docs/comms-off-bees.png)

**Eagle meetings: filtering competing priorities.** Three times on the journey (and once more during the build), other eagles fly in above the canopy and circle the Leader,
each with a priority card. The Leader weighs them up, says ✅ **yes** to the one that helps the team reach the pyramid,
and **parks** the others ("Not now; the pyramid comes first"). The visitors fly off, and the Leader puts the beam back on the vision ("Keep it going").
The top bar counts **Priorities filtered**.

| Meeting | Visiting eagles and their asks | ✅ Accepted (supports the pyramid) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 💰 Finance: "Budget cuts?" · 🎨 Marketing: "Urgent: rebrand!" | 🦺 Safety: "Safety gear for koalas" |
| 2 | 🏛️ Board: "Can you also run the picnic?" · 📁 Other project: "Lend us two koalas?" | 🗺️ Product: "Better map to the pyramid" |
| 3 | 🚀 Strategy: "New initiative!" · 👥 HR: "Can you also do a survey?" | 🤝 Customers: "Pyramid opening: still on?" |
| 4 (mid-build) | 🛍️ Sales: "Add a gift shop to the pyramid?" | None: "After the launch! 🛡️ Let the koalas finish." |

![An eagle meeting: the Leader accepts one priority and parks the others](docs/eagle-meeting-priorities.png)

**Surprises: things nobody saw coming.** Three events happen that the Manager *couldn't* have spotted, even with communication ON.
They're marked with a pulsing orange **❓ UNPLANNED** sign and a red **SURPRISE** caption tag.
The koalas stop and report **up the chain**: koalas ➜ Manager ("Manager! A sinkhole just opened up!"), then Manager ➜ Leader
("Unplanned sinkhole. Team needs planks, about a week."). The Leader answers ("Safety first, take the week. Goal stays the same."),
the answer travels back down, and the team solves it together. The top bar counts **Surprises reported up**.

| # | Surprise | Reported up and answered | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🕳️ Sinkhole opens in the track | "Safety first, take the week. Goal stays the same." | Gather planks from the bush and lay them across |
| 2 | 🌳 A gum tree falls across the path | "Yes! Sending saws and safety gear down now. 🪂" (a supply drop lands) | Saw it up with the new gear and roll it away |
| 3 | 🐍 Big brown snake on the track | "No rush. Let it pass safely. Goal stays the same." | Wait quietly until it slithers off |

When a surprise is handled, the caption says how many days it cost ("The sinkhole cost 7 days, but everyone knew why and the goal stayed the same").

With communication OFF, the koalas' calls go unanswered ("...hello?"). They handle each surprise alone, with a false start and a slower fix.

### How long is a run? (days and minutes)
Project time: **1 project day = 2 seconds of animation at 1× speed.**

| | Project time | Real time at 1× | Unwarned obstacles | Wrong turns | Surprises reported up | Manager syncs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Communication **ON** | **about 157 days** | about 5 min (312 s) | 0 | 0 | 3 | 3 |
| Communication **OFF** | **about 202 days** | about 6¾ min (404 s) | 8 | 13 | 0 | 3 |

With communication ON the run also has 1 team idea adopted (the creek route saves 4 to 6 days) and 1 rest break (about 4 days).
With communication OFF there's no idea and no break, but the exhausted koalas slow right down.
The build itself (including the climb back down) takes about 36 days with communication ON and about 48 days OFF (the wrong-shaped course has to be redone).

The finish summary shows "Pyramid built in N days" and compares it with the other mode: your own result if you've run both in this session, otherwise the typical figure.
For example: "Without communication the same project usually takes about 202 days: 45 days lost."

## How it's built

- **Blazor WebAssembly** (standalone, .NET 10) for the page, controls, captions and role info cards (`src/AboveTheCanopy/Pages/Home.razor`, `Models/RoleInfo.cs`).
- A small **HTML5 canvas engine** in plain JavaScript (`wwwroot/js/canopy.js`) for smooth 60 fps animation, loaded as a module through JS interop.
- All the art is drawn with canvas shapes. There are no images, no external libraries and no CDNs, so it works offline.

```
AboveTheCanopy.sln
src/AboveTheCanopy/
  Pages/Home.razor        UI: canvas, controls, caption bar, info cards, summary
  Models/RoleInfo.cs      Teaching text for each role
  Models/Chapters.cs      The 24 chapters: story mark, title, teaching point, questions
  wwwroot/js/canopy.js    Animation engine: scene, agents, story, perspectives, chapters
  wwwroot/js/sound.js     Procedural Web Audio sound effects and ambience
  wwwroot/css/app.css     Styling
deploy/pages.yml             GitHub Pages workflow (move to .github/workflows/ to enable)
```

Want to change the story? Everything is in `canopy.js`:
- `OB_DEFS` sets the obstacle layout, and `sequenceFor()` holds the obstacle scripts, with an "informed" and an "uninformed" version of each.
- `DISTRACTIONS` holds the leader and manager distractions (when they trigger, and what they say).
- `MEETINGS` holds the eagle meetings: visiting eagles, their asks, which one is accepted, and the Leader's answers.
- `MGR_MEETINGS` holds the manager-level owl meetings (which obstacle they happen at, the visiting owl's colours and the conversation).
- `SEC_PER_DAY` sets the project-time scale (2 s = 1 day). `GATHER_SPOTS` and `gatherPose()` control how the koalas fan out to gather materials.
- `SURPRISE_DEFS` holds the unplanned events and the full up-the-chain conversation for each one. `surpriseSeq()` and `upChain()` run them.
- `LEADER_TALK`, `MANAGER_CHECKIN`, `RECOGNITION` and `CHEERS` hold the routine messages.
- `WALK_SPEED_INFORMED` / `WALK_SPEED_UNINFORMED` and `makePathBushes()` control the pacing.
- `KOALAS` holds the koalas' names, colours and hats. `COACH_X`, `LANTANA`, `CREEK` and `BREAK_X` place the coaching moment, the lantana patch, the creek route and the rest break.
- `coachSeq()`, `startIdea()` / `adoptIdea()`, `startRecognition()` and `startBreak()` run the v5 moments. `whenFree()` waits until the Manager is free (no meeting, distraction or task) before starting one, so they never talk over each other.
- `mark()` records story moments; a chapter starts at the first moment named in `Models/Chapters.cs`. `jumpTo()` restarts and fast-forwards (fixed 1/60 s steps, seeded randomness) to a chapter.
- `sound.js` holds every sound as a small synth recipe (`SFX`), the ambient forest bed (`buildAmbience()`, `tick()`), the per-sound rate limits (`RATE`) and the saved on/off and volume settings. `audioTick()` and `ambLevels()` in `canopy.js` connect it to the story.
- `caption()` queues captions with a priority and an optional hold (`pumpCaptions()` shows them), so meeting and surprise captions are never cut off.

Note: .NET 10 fingerprints static files at build time, so after editing `canopy.js` outside Visual Studio, rebuild (or use `dotnet watch`).
