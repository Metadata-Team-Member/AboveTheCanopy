# 🌳 Above the Canopy

**An interactive animation about the difference between leadership, management and team members.**

A large Australian forest with a tall canopy. A pyramid pokes out of the top of the trees far to the east: that's the goal.
Time is measured like a real project, in **days**: the top bar counts *Day 1, Day 2...* until the koalas deliver.

| Role | Character | Where they are | What they can see | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leader** | 🦅 Eagle | Flies *above* the canopy | Where the team started, and the pyramid (the goal) | **Vision & direction.** Sets the destination, keeps everyone pointed at it, filters competing priorities, accepts unavoidable delays, recognises progress |
| **Manager** | 🦉 Owl | Flies *below* the canopy, above the team | The obstacles ahead | **Planning, removing obstacles, communication.** Scouts ahead, warns the team, arranges training, coordinates with other managers, escalates surprises, reports progress |
| **Team** | 🐨 Four koalas | On the forest floor, cutting through the bush | Only a few metres ahead | **Execution & problem-solving.** The everyday work, gathering materials, learning new skills, solving every obstacle, and reporting the surprises nobody planned for |

![The koalas hacking through the bush, the owl scouting, the eagle above the canopy](docs/koalas-hacking-bushes.png)

## The teaching idea

Nobody can see the whole picture on their own:

- The **leader** can see the goal but not the boulders.
- The **manager** can see the boulders but not the goal.
- The **team** can see neither. They're down in the bush doing the work.

The journey works when vision flows down (leader ➜ manager), warnings and plans flow down to the team (manager ➜ team), and requests and progress flow back up (team ➜ manager ➜ leader).
Use the **Perspective** buttons to see the world through each role's eyes, and turn **Manager communication OFF**
to watch the same koalas in the same forest get surprised, take wrong turns and take much longer (about 140 days instead of about 105).

## What happens on the journey

**The everyday work.** Between the big obstacles, the koalas keep stopping to cut through small bushes on the path: little pruning saws swinging, leaves flying.
That's the constant day-to-day work that nobody above the canopy sees.

**Eight obstacles,** each one different, each with a warned solution and a "nobody told us" version:

| # | Obstacle | When the Manager warned them | When communication is OFF |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🌿 Tangle of vines | Arrive with cutting tools and cut straight through | Get stuck, run back to camp for better tools |
| 2 | 🪨 Giant boulder | **Upskill:** a quick hammer-and-chisel training session, then break the rock down together | Bonk into it, push, back-track, then teach themselves by trial and error (slowly) |
| 3 | 🌊 Fast river | **Gather** logs from the bush and build a bridge | One koala wades in (splash!), then they scatter hunting for anything that floats |
| 4 | 🟫 Smelly bog | **Gather** flat stones and lay stepping stones | One koala sinks to its tummy, then they wander off for rocks and test wobbly stones |
| 5 | 🪵 Fallen giant log | Crawl through its hollow | Try to climb over it, slide off, trek along it |
| 6 | ⛰️ Cliff edge | **Gather** long vines, braid a rope, abseil at the marked spot | Wander the edge, then hunt for anything rope-like |
| 7 | 🐝 Bees' nest | Tiptoe past in silence | Stomp underneath, get chased by the swarm |
| 8 | 🧗 Rock wall | **Gather** straight poles and lash a ladder | Slide down bare-pawed, hunt for sticks, lash together a wobbly ladder |

When an obstacle is cleared, the caption says how many days it took. Unwarned obstacles also say what it would have taken
("Rock wall cleared, but going in blind took 6 days instead of about 3 days").

**Upskilling at the boulder: investing in team skills.** The boulder is too big to go around. With communication ON, the Manager's warning
comes with training: a whiteboard reads *🔨 HAMMER & CHISEL 101*, lightbulbs pop over the koalas, and they earn a 🎓 **UPSKILLED!** badge.
Then they spread around the rock and break it down together: rhythmic tapping, chips flying, cracks spreading, the boulder splitting into chunks,
then rubble they step over. With communication OFF, they bonk into it, push, back-track, and finally teach themselves (*TRIAL & ERROR...*, *SELF-TAUGHT*), much more slowly.

![Upskilled koalas break the boulder down with hammers and chisels](docs/boulder-hammer-chisel.png)

**Gathering materials.** When the koalas need logs (river bridge), flat stones (bog), planks (sinkhole), vines (cliff rope) or poles (ladder),
they fan out off the path into the bush, pick the materials up and carry them back in their arms, where a pile builds up and is used during the build.
With communication OFF they wander further, unsure what they're even looking for.

![Koalas carrying logs back to the river while the Manager meets an Ops manager owl](docs/gathering-logs-manager-meeting.png)

**Distractions happen, so refocus.** The Leader and the Manager get distracted too, veer off course, then refocus on the goal.
Each distraction is narrated, and the top bar counts **Distractions handled**:

- 🦅 Leader: swooping magpies, an "URGENT!!!" reply-all email storm, a storm front and wind gust.
- 🦉 Manager: a "Sync about the sync" meeting request, a cloud of mozzies, a snag of branches and vines.

![A magpie swoops the eagle](docs/leader-distraction-magpie.png)

**Manager meetings: sideways coordination.** Three times, while the koalas are busy gathering materials, another owl flies in under the canopy
for a short manager-level meeting (caption tag **MANAGER MEETING**). A dashed 🤝 link joins the two owls while they talk, then the visitor flies off
and the Manager refocuses on the team (a green line re-locks onto the koalas). The top bar counts **🤝 Manager syncs**.

| Visiting owl | Topic | The conversation |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Ops manager | Sharing resources | "Your team needs logs? We have spare!" · "Yes please! Drop them by the river." |
| ⚪ PMO | Status sync | "Status sync?" · "River done, bog in progress. On track." · "Great, I'll update the roadmap." |
| 🟠 Project manager B | Coordinating between teams | "Can we share your rope after?" · "Sure, once my koalas are down the cliff." · "Need a ladder later?" |

**Lots of communication.** The leader checks in with the manager and gets progress reports after every obstacle.
The manager briefs the koalas and checks on them. The koalas ask for things ("Need more rope!", "Pass more stones down the line!") and get answers.
Wins get recognised ("Great work, koalas!") and celebrated. With communication OFF, the koalas' calls go unanswered ("Is anyone up there?").

![Communication OFF: the koalas walk under the bees' nest](docs/comms-off-bees.png)

**Eagle meetings: filtering competing priorities.** Three times on the journey, other eagles fly in above the canopy and circle the Leader,
each with a priority card. The Leader weighs them up, says ✅ **yes** to the one that helps the team reach the pyramid,
and **parks** the others ("Not now; the pyramid comes first"). The visitors fly off, and the Leader puts the beam back on the goal ("Keep it going").
The top bar counts **Priorities filtered**.

| Meeting | Visiting eagles and their asks | ✅ Accepted (supports the pyramid) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 💰 Finance: "Budget cuts?" · 🎨 Marketing: "Urgent: rebrand!" | 🦺 Safety: "Safety gear for koalas" |
| 2 | 🏛️ Board: "Can you also run the picnic?" · 📁 Other project: "Lend us two koalas?" | 🗺️ Product: "Better map to the pyramid" |
| 3 | 🚀 Strategy: "New initiative!" · 👥 HR: "Can you also do a survey?" | 🤝 Customers: "Pyramid opening: still on?" |

![An eagle meeting: the Leader accepts one priority and parks the others](docs/eagle-meeting-priorities.png)

**Surprises: things nobody saw coming.** Three events happen that the Manager *couldn't* have spotted, even with communication ON.
They're marked with a pulsing orange **❓ UNPLANNED** sign and a red **SURPRISE** caption tag.
The koalas stop and report **up the chain**: koalas ➜ Manager ("Manager! A sinkhole just opened up!"), then Manager ➜ Leader
("Unplanned sinkhole. Team needs planks, about a week."). The Leader answers ("Safety first, take the week. Goal stays the same."),
the answer travels back down, and the team solves it together. The top bar counts **Surprises reported up**.

| # | Surprise | Reported up and answered | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🕳️ Sinkhole opens in the track | "Safety first, take the week. Goal stays the same." | Gather planks from the bush and lay them across |
| 2 | 🌳 A gum tree falls across the path | "Everyone safe? Then take the days. We'll adjust the plan." | Saw it up and roll it away |
| 3 | 🐍 Big brown snake on the track | "No rush. Let it pass safely. Goal stays the same." | Wait quietly until it slithers off |

When a surprise is handled, the caption says how many days it cost ("The sinkhole cost 7 days, but everyone knew why and the goal stayed the same").

With communication OFF, the koalas' calls go unanswered ("...hello?"). They handle each surprise alone, with a false start and a slower fix.

![A surprise reported up the chain: koalas to Manager to Leader, and back down](docs/surprise-up-the-chain.png)

### How long is a run? (days and minutes)
Project time: **1 project day = 2 seconds of animation at 1× speed.**

| | Project time | Real time at 1× | Unwarned obstacles | Wrong turns | Surprises reported up | Manager syncs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Communication **ON** | **about 105 days** | about 3½ min (210 s) | 0 | 0 | 3 | 3 |
| Communication **OFF** | **about 140 days** | about 4⅔ min (278 s) | 8 | 12 | 0 | 3 |

The finish summary shows "Delivered in N days" and compares it with the other mode: your own result if you've run both in this session, otherwise the typical figure.
For example: "Without communication the same project usually takes about 140 days: 35 days lost."

![Finish summary: delivered in 105 days, compared with communication OFF](docs/finish-days.png)
Use the speed slider (0.25× to 3×) to fit your session.

### Discussion prompts
1. What could the leader see that nobody else could? What couldn't they see?
2. When communication was off, what changed for the koalas? Was it their fault?
3. Most of the koalas' time went on small bushes, not big obstacles. What's the "bush hacking" in your team's week?
4. What are your magpies, meeting requests and reply-all storms? How do you get back to the goal?
5. In your organisation, who is the "manager under the canopy"? How do the warnings reach the team, and how do requests get back up?
6. Which "Can you also...?" requests land on your leaders? How do they decide what to park?
7. When something nobody planned for happens, how fast does the news travel up, and does the answer come back down?
8. The boulder only broke once the koalas learned a new skill. Where would an hour of training save your team days?
9. Who are the "other owls" your managers coordinate with? What do they share?

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| ▶ Play / ⏸ Pause | Start or stop the animation (keyboard: <kbd>Space</kbd>) |
| ↻ Restart | Start the journey again (<kbd>R</kbd>) |
| Speed slider | 0.25× to 3× |
| Perspective: Everything / Leader / Manager / Team | Fogs out what that role **can't** see (<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd>) |
| Manager communication ON/OFF | When OFF, the manager still spots obstacles but never tells the koalas |
| Click the eagle, owl, koalas or pyramid | Opens an info card about that role |

The caption bar narrates each phase (with a red **SURPRISE** tag for unplanned events and an amber tag for meetings). The stats at the top count:
**📅 Day** (project time), **⚠️ Unwarned obstacles** (planned obstacles the koalas hit with no warning), **↩ Wrong turns**, **🧘 Distractions handled**,
**🗂️ Priorities filtered** (asks the Leader parked or declined), **📣 Surprises reported up** and **🤝 Manager syncs**.
The info cards scroll (with a visible scrollbar), so all the text is readable in a small window.

![An info card scrolling in a small window](docs/info-card-scroll.png)
When the team reaches the pyramid, a summary lets you replay with communication flipped so you can compare.

## Run it

### Visual Studio
1. Clone: `git clone https://github.com/Metadata-Team-Member/AboveTheCanopy.git`
2. Open **`AboveTheCanopy.sln`** in Visual Studio 2026, or any Visual Studio version with the **.NET 10 SDK** and the *ASP.NET and web development* workload.
3. Press **F5**. The browser opens at `http://localhost:5131`.

### Command line
```bash
dotnet run --project src/AboveTheCanopy
# then open http://localhost:5131
```

### Online
**https://metadata-team-member.github.io/AboveTheCanopy/**

Right now the site is served from the `gh-pages` branch, which holds the published `wwwroot` output.
To publish automatically on every push to `main`, move `deploy/pages.yml` to `.github/workflows/pages.yml`
(pushing workflow files needs a token with the `workflow` scope), then set **Settings → Pages → Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
The workflow publishes the app, sets `<base href="/AboveTheCanopy/">`, and adds `.nojekyll` and a `404.html`.

## How it's built

- **Blazor WebAssembly** (standalone, .NET 10) for the page, controls, captions and role info cards (`src/AboveTheCanopy/Pages/Home.razor`, `Models/RoleInfo.cs`).
- A small **HTML5 canvas engine** in plain JavaScript (`wwwroot/js/canopy.js`) for smooth 60 fps animation, loaded as a module through JS interop.
- All the art is drawn with canvas shapes. There are no images, no external libraries and no CDNs, so it works offline.

```
AboveTheCanopy.sln
src/AboveTheCanopy/
  Pages/Home.razor        UI: canvas, controls, caption bar, info cards, summary
  Models/RoleInfo.cs      Teaching text for each role
  wwwroot/js/canopy.js    Animation engine: scene, agents, story, perspectives
  wwwroot/css/app.css     Styling
deploy/pages.yml             GitHub Pages workflow (move to .github/workflows/ to enable)
```

Want to change the story? Everything is in `canopy.js`:
- `OB_DEFS` sets the obstacle layout, and `sequenceFor()` holds the obstacle scripts, with an "informed" and an "uninformed" version of each.
- `DISTRACTIONS` holds the leader and manager distractions (when they trigger, and what they say).
- `MEETINGS` holds the eagle meetings: visiting eagles, their asks, which one is accepted, and the Leader's answers.
- `MGR_MEETINGS` holds the manager-level owl meetings (which obstacle they happen at, the visiting owl's colours and the conversation).
- `SEC_PER_DAY` sets the project-time scale (2 s = 1 day). `GATHER_SPOTS` and `gatherPose()` control how the koalas fan out to gather materials.
- `SURPRISE_DEFS` holds the unplanned events and the full up-the-chain conversation for each one. `surpriseSeq()` and `upChain()` run them.
- `LEADER_TALK`, `MANAGER_CHECKIN`, `RECOGNITION` and `CHEERS` hold the routine messages.
- `WALK_SPEED_INFORMED` / `WALK_SPEED_UNINFORMED` and `makePathBushes()` control the pacing.

Note: .NET 10 fingerprints static files at build time, so after editing `canopy.js` outside Visual Studio, rebuild (or use `dotnet watch`).
